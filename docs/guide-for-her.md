# The Habitbot Handbook 🦦💧

Hi! Someone who loves you built you a little companion. It lives in WhatsApp, and its whole job is taking care of you — water, vitamins, reminders, and occasionally saying exactly what you need to hear. This is everything it can do.

## Day 1 — it introduces itself

The first time it texts you, it'll ask three things:

1. **Its personality** — Sassy 😈 (dramatic, will roast you lovingly), Sweet 🥰 (soft and endlessly encouraging), or Pet 🦦 (a tiny otter named Bubbles whose happiness depends on your hydration).
2. **Language** — English or Hinglish.
3. **Your awake hours** — it promises to never text outside them.

Nothing is permanent. Any day, just tell it: **"be nicer"**, **"roast me"**, or **"pet mode"** — it switches instantly. Same for language.

## What it does every day

- **Morning hello** — today's plan, your streaks, and the actual weather (on hot days it'll lobby for extra glasses).
- **Water, paced to your day** — 8 glasses across your waking hours. Here's the deal: **if you're on track, it stays completely silent.** It only speaks up when you fall behind, max a handful of messages a day, never at night.
- **Vitamin days** — every other day, so you never have to remember which day it is. It knows.
- **If you ignore it…** it gets progressively more dramatic (this is a feature). After its "final word" it drops the subject for the day. It can take a hint.
- **Sunday 8pm** — your weekly report card: a grade, your best day, points earned, and next week's challenge.

## Logging is one tap (or one word)

- Tap the buttons: **[💧 +1 glass] [Snooze 1h] [Skip today]**
- Or just text it: `done` · `2` · `had 2 glasses` · `ho gaya` — it understands you.

## Skip and snooze, guilt-free

- **`snooze`** — it comes back in an hour.
- **`skip`** — today doesn't count *against* you. Your streak **freezes instead of breaking**. Skips are honest days, not failures.
- **`rough day`** or **`not today`** — instant gentle mode: no drama, almost no reminders, until tomorrow morning. It takes this seriously.

## Streaks, points, and actual prizes

- Completing a habit builds its **streak**; completing everything makes a **perfect day**; everything earns **points**.
- At streak milestones (3 days, 7 days, 14…) you unlock **rewards — real ones, funded by Rishabh**. Massages, dinners, no-questions-asked favors. Some arrive with a voice note. 👀
- Text **`redeem`** anytime to see what you've earned and cash it in. He gets notified. He has to pay up. This is legally binding (emotionally).

## It's also your personal reminder assistant

- **"remind me to call mom at 5"** · **"remind me about the parcel tomorrow morning"** — it confirms, then pings you at the time (give or take a few minutes).
- **`reminders`** — see everything queued.
- **`cancel reminder 3`** — cancel one.
- These always come through — even in gentle mode.

## Just… talk to it

- **`status`** — your day at a glance: progress, streaks, points.
- Chat with it. It has opinions. It remembers your vibe.
- Yes, you can ask it about Rishabh. Go ahead. Try to trick it. See what happens.

## Fine print

- It sleeps when you sleep — zero messages outside your chosen hours.
- Reminders arrive within a few minutes of the time you asked for.
- If it ever feels like too much: `snooze`, `skip`, or `rough day` — it genuinely listens. (Or complain to the management. The management is very motivated to keep you happy.)

*Built with an unreasonable amount of love.* 💙
