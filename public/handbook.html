Every day

The rhythm

  • Morning hello — today's plan, your streaks, and the real weather (hot days = it lobbies for bonus glasses).
  • Water, paced to your day — and here's the deal: when you're on track, it stays completely silent. It only pipes up when you fall behind.
  • Vitamin days — every other day. You never have to remember which day it is; it knows.
  • Ignore it and it gets theatrical — progressively more dramatic (this is a feature), then it says its final word and drops it for the day. It can take a hint.
  • Sunday 8pm — your weekly report card: a grade, your best day, points, and next week's challenge.

No guilt allowed

Skip, snooze, rough days

  • snooze — it comes back in an hour.
  • skip — today doesn't count against you. Your streak freezes instead of breaking. Skips are honest days, not failures.
  • rough day — instant gentle mode until tomorrow morning: no drama, barely any reminders. It takes this one seriously.
The good part
